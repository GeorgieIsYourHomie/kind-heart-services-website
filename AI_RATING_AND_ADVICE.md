## 💡 Recommendations for Enhancement

### High Priority

1. **Add Page-Specific Metadata**

   - Each page (About, Services, Contact) should have unique meta titles and descriptions
   - This improves SEO and click-through rates

2. **Implement Structured Data (JSON-LD)**

   - Add LocalBusiness schema markup
   - Include organization details, address, phone, hours
   - Helps with Google Business Profile integration

3. **Add Open Graph Tags**

   - Improves social media sharing appearance
   - Better engagement on Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.

4. **Create FAQ Section**

   - Addresses common questions families have
   - Great for SEO (featured snippets potential)
   - Reduces support burden

5. **Add More Social Proof**
   - More testimonials (aim for 5-7)
   - Consider adding reviews/ratings if available
   - Add certifications or awards

### Medium Priority

6. **Add Blog/News Section**

   - Regular content helps SEO
   - Positions as thought leader
   - Topics: care tips, facility updates, community events

7. **Enhance Services Page**

   - Add pricing information (if appropriate)
   - Add downloadable service brochures
   - More detailed service descriptions

8. **Improve Contact Form**

   - Add form validation with clear error messages
   - Add success/error states
   - Consider adding preferred contact method
   - Add "How did you hear about us?" field

9. **Add Virtual Tour/Photo Gallery**

   - Visual representation of facility
   - Helps families visualize the space
   - Can be simple image gallery or 360° tour

10. **Add Staff/Team Section**
    - Introduces key staff members
    - Builds trust and personal connection
    - Shows qualifications and experience

### Low Priority (Nice to Have)

11. **Add Resources Section**

    - Downloadable guides (e.g., "Choosing an Assisted Living Facility")
    - Links to helpful resources
    - Care planning tools

12. **Add Events Calendar**

    - Shows community engagement
    - Demonstrates active community
    - Can be simple list or calendar view

13. **Add Newsletter Signup**

    - Builds email list
    - Keeps families informed
    - Marketing opportunity

14. **Add Live Chat**
    - Immediate response to questions
    - Can increase conversions
    - Consider chatbot for after-hours
